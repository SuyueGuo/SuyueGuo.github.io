I'm a Ph.D. student at [UCSB SecLab](https://seclab.cs.ucsb.edu/members/), advised by [Prof. Giovanni Vigna](https://sites.cs.ucsb.edu/~vigna/) and [Prof. Christopher Kruegel](https://sites.cs.ucsb.edu/~chris/).

My research focuses on **building trustworthy software systems in the age of AI**. I pursue this goal from two complementary directions: leveraging AI to discover software flaws and improve software quality, and understanding and improving the correctness and reliability of AI-generated software and agentic systems. My work spans LLM-assisted fuzzing, automated software testing, and the evaluation of AI agents in software engineering workflows.

I have discovered over 30 previously unknown bugs in widely used software, including Adobe Acrobat Reader, Foxit PDF Reader, PDF-XChange Editor, and Vim, and worked with developers to address them. I have also participated in CTF competitions as a member of [Shellphish](https://shellphish.net/).
